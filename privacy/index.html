Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Kronos Tech: our website kronos1993.github.io and the software we publish.

EFFECTIVE DATE · OCTOBER 16, 2026

1. Who We Are

Kronos Tech ("we," "us," or "our") is an independent software developer that builds and publishes applications for Android, iOS, macOS and Windows. This policy explains what information we handle, why, and the choices you have.

Our principle: your data stays with you. We don't sell personal information, we don't require accounts, and our apps keep your content on your own device by default.

2. App-Specific Policies

Each of our apps has its own privacy policy describing exactly what that app does. Where an app-specific policy is more detailed than this one, the app-specific policy applies:

  • Lumina Sound: music and podcast player (Android, iOS, macOS, Windows)
  • Weather app: developed by Kronos Tech and currently published on Google Play under the CarebridgePro developer account; its own policy applies to it
  • Pokédex

3. Our Website

Our website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and it has no forms or user accounts.

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to deliver and secure the service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Fonts: pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Language preference: if you switch between English and Spanish, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.
  • Downloads: desktop installers are served from GitHub Releases, subject to GitHub's privacy statement.

4. Information Handled by Our Apps

Our apps are designed to work without an account. Depending on the app and the features you use, the following may apply (each app's policy gives the details):

  • Content on your device: files, libraries, settings and history are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to us.
  • Purchases: in-app purchases are processed by the platform store (Google Play, Apple App Store, Microsoft Store) or, for direct desktop downloads, by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record, sometimes through RevenueCat. We never receive your full payment details. We may receive the email address and order or license information needed to deliver and support your purchase.
  • Location: apps that need your location to work (such as Weather app showing your local forecast) ask for permission first. The location is sent only to the data provider needed to answer the request, is not stored by us, and you can revoke the permission at any time in your device settings.
  • Advertising and analytics: only the free mobile version of Lumina Sound shows ads (Google AdMob) and uses Firebase Analytics, and only according to the consent choices you make in the app. Its desktop versions and our other apps contain no ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Online features you choose to use: features such as lyrics lookup, podcast feeds or scrobbling send only the request data those services need (for example, a song title) directly to the respective provider.
  • Emails you send us: if you contact us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties for their own marketing.

5. Data Security

We protect the information we handle with appropriate measures for its nature:

  • All network communication from our apps and website uses encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Data stays on your device whenever possible, which minimizes what could ever be exposed. Where an app stores sensitive data locally, it uses the platform's protections (for example, database encryption).
  • Payments and license management are handled by established, PCI-compliant providers. We never store payment card data.
  • Access to our developer and publishing accounts is restricted and protected with strong authentication.
  • Software is distributed only through official stores and our own release channels.

6. Reporting a Security Issue

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in any of our apps or on our website, please email us at kronostech.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Security report" and enough detail to reproduce it. We will acknowledge your report, investigate it promptly, and keep you informed of the fix. Please give us reasonable time to address the issue before disclosing it publicly.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Because our apps keep data on your device, you control it directly: you can clear an app's data or uninstall the app at any time. Consent for ads and analytics can be changed from within the apps that use them.

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, such as emails you sent us or purchase support records. Contact us at the address below and we will respond within the time required by law.

8. Children's Privacy

Our software is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
